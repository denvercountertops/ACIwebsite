Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information American Cabinetry Inc. (“we”) collects through this website and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you request a consultation, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, phone number, email, the type of customer you are, your project details, your city or zip code, your message, and any photo, plan or drawing you upload. If you call or email us, we receive the information you share with us that way.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, schedule your consultation, prepare designs and quotes, and follow up about your project. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Countertop requests

If you tell us you also need countertops, we may share your contact and project details with our sister companies, Denver Countertops and Stone Ideas, so they can help with that part of your project.

Services that help run this site

Consultation requests are delivered to our email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). This site is hosted by Netlify, and its fonts are provided by Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when you visit the site or send the form.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep consultation requests for as long as we need them to serve you and run our business. To ask what information we have about you, or to ask us to delete it, contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
