Frameless Construction

What does frameless mean?

Frameless, or European-style, cabinets are built without a face frame on the front of the box. Doors and drawers attach directly to the cabinet sides. The result:

  • More storage. Full-width openings with no frame in the way.

  • Easier access. Wider drawers and nothing to catch on.

  • A cleaner look. Tight, even gaps for a modern, seamless finish.

  • Flexible style. Works with slab, shaker and other door styles.
