Our Cabinets

European-Style Cabinets, Built Your Way

Every cabinet we sell is built in our own shop. Our frameless, European-style construction gives you clean lines and more usable storage. Choose semi-custom for speed and value, or fully custom when your space calls for something one of a kind.

Choose your level: in-stock RTA, semi-custom or fully custom cabinets

  • Ready now

    In-Stock RTA

    Ready-to-assemble cabinets built in our shop in popular sizes and finishes, in stock and ready to go. A quick, budget-friendly way to get your project moving.

    Good for: DIY projects, rentals and flips, laundry rooms and garages, contractors on tight timelines.

  • Faster & budget-friendly

    Semi-Custom

    Built in our shop from proven sizes and styles, with your choice of material, finish, hardware and modifications. A faster, budget-friendly way to get quality cabinetry.

    Good for: kitchen refreshes, rentals, multi-unit projects, builders who need consistency.

  • One of a kind

    Fully Custom

    Designed and built to your exact dimensions and layout. Ideal for unusual spaces, specialty storage and one-of-a-kind designs.

    Good for: dream kitchens, built-ins, designer-led projects, high-end homes.

Choose your material: melamine, laminate or wood

  • Melamine

    Durable, easy to clean and a great value. A smart choice for kitchens, laundry rooms, closets, garages and commercial spaces.

  • Laminate

    A wide range of colors, textures and woodgrain looks. Holds up to daily wear and moisture, making it a strong choice for busy kitchens and bathrooms.

  • Wood

    Warm and timeless, finished in stain or paint. Real wood brings natural character that gets better with age.

What does frameless mean?

Frameless, or European-style, cabinets are built without a face frame on the front of the box. Doors and drawers attach directly to the cabinet sides. The result:

  • More storage. Full-width openings with no frame in the way.

  • Easier access. Wider drawers and nothing to catch on.

  • A cleaner look. Tight, even gaps for a modern, seamless finish.

  • Flexible style. Works with slab, shaker and other door styles.

Not sure which is right for you? Book a free consultation and we’ll walk you through samples, options and design.

FAQ

Common questions about our cabinets

What’s the difference between semi-custom and custom?

Semi-custom starts from proven sizes and styles with options to personalize; fully custom is built to your exact design and dimensions. Both are built in our shop.

What are frameless cabinets?

European-style cabinets with no face frame, which gives you more storage space and a clean, modern look.

Which material should I choose?

It depends on budget, style and use. Melamine and laminate are durable and great value; wood offers a warm, classic look. We’ll help you compare samples during your free consultation.

Can I see samples before deciding?

Yes. During your free consultation we’ll walk you through samples of door styles, materials and finishes.

Do you work with contractors and builders?

Yes, we work with homeowners, contractors, builders, developers, designers and commercial clients.

Do you also do countertops?

Yes, through our sister companies Denver Countertops (opens in a new tab) and Stone Ideas (opens in a new tab).

How much do cabinets cost?

Every project is different. Price depends on size, material, door style and level of customization. Book a free consultation for an accurate quote.
