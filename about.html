Who We Are

Cabinets built in-house, from our shop to your space

American Cabinetry Inc. builds frameless, European-style cabinets in our Commerce City, Colorado shop, in semi-custom and fully custom options. We work with homeowners, contractors, builders, developers, designers and commercial clients across the Denver area.

Because we build in-house, you talk with the people who design and make your cabinets, from the first consultation to the final walkthrough. And through our sister companies, Denver Countertops (opens in a new tab) and Stone Ideas (opens in a new tab), we can coordinate your countertops too.
